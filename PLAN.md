# Yomi - Manga Reader App

## Yomi - Manga Flow par Okitakoy Corp

### Design
- **Theme**: Material You (dynamic) with manual override options: Light, Dark, and Sepia (warm beige/brown for night reading)
- **Colors**: Deep purple to electric cyan accent gradient, with adaptive Material You palettes
- **Typography**: Material 3 type scale, clean and readable
- **Layout**: Bottom navigation with 5 tabs (Browse, Library, History, Downloads, Settings), plus a navigation drawer for additional options including FAQ

### Features
- **Browse / Home**: Discover manga by categories (Popular, Latest, Updated), featured carousel, category chips
- **Library**: Personal manga collection with favorites, reading status filters, and grid/list toggle
- **History**: Recently read manga with progress tracking
- **Downloads**: Offline manga chapters with download management and queue
- **Reader**: Dual-page mode, webtoon (vertical scroll) mode, zoom, brightness control, reading direction toggle
- **Sources**: Manage manga sources, enable/disable, add custom URL JSON sources
- **Settings**: Theme selector (System/Light/Dark/Sepia), sync options, reader preferences, notifications
- **FAQ / About**: Dedicated "Okitakoy Corp" page with company info, FAQ questions, credits. Accessible from drawer menu.
- **Splash Screen**: Branded launch with Yomi logo and "Manga Flow par Okitakoy Corp" subtitle

### Screens
1. **Splash Screen** - Branded launch
2. **Home / Browse** - Discover manga categories and featured titles
3. **Library** - User's saved manga collection
4. **History** - Reading history with progress
5. **Downloads** - Offline chapters manager
6. **Manga Detail** - Title info, chapters list, start reading, add to favorites
7. **Reader** - Full-screen manga reader with mode toggles
8. **Sources** - Source management screen
9. **Settings** - App preferences and theme
10. **FAQ / About** - Okitakoy Corp info and FAQ

### App Icon
- Vector-drawn eye with manga-page pupil, purple-to-cyan gradient on dark background
